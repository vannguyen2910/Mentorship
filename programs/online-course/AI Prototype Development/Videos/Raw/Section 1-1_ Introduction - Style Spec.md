# Section 1-1: Introduction — Style Spec

Built from your Section 1 cover art (sampled directly: background reads #27184A in the corners, warming to about #2F1D56 near center — a soft radial gradient, not flat) plus the three Pinterest references. Two caption registers, used deliberately rather than blended.

## Colors
- Background / card fill: `#27184A` → `#2F1D56` radial gradient (matches the cover exactly)
- Primary text/line color: white, matching the cover's line-art weight — not a heavy black outline
- Emphasis highlight block (for the two "big moment" beats only): solid block behind the key phrase, same navy-purple family so it stays on-brand rather than the stock lime-green from the Pinterest reference — use a lighter tint, around `#4A3580`, so it reads as a highlight against the darker background

## Caption style 1 — Default (runs under ~90% of the video)
Reference: Pinterest pin 3 (Edward Kennedy) restraint, not pins 1/2's energy.
- White, medium-bold weight, single size throughout — no word-by-word size jumps
- Bottom-third placement, no background box
- Simple cut/fade between caption lines, no bounce or pop-in animation
- This is what CapCut's auto-caption gives you by default if you just pick a clean sans preset — don't reach for a "trendy" template here

## Caption style 2 — Emphasis (only at the 2 flagged cut-map beats)
Reference: Pinterest pins 1 & 2 (word-size hierarchy + highlight block), recolored to the palette above.
- Bold/black weight sans-serif
- The key phrase or number sized noticeably larger than the surrounding words — e.g. "50+ designers" or "hệ thống, không phải may rủi" gets the blow-up treatment, not the whole sentence
- Highlight block (`#4A3580`) behind the emphasized phrase only
- Used exactly twice in this video: segment 2 (mentoring stat) and segment 5 (course thesis line) from the cut map — nowhere else

## Opening identity card
Reference: Pinterest pin 3's lower-third layout.
- Name in bold caps, thin white rule underneath, title line below in a lighter weight — "WINNIE NGUYEN" / "UX PRODUCT DESIGNER"
- Sits over the talking head briefly during the opening line, then clears — not a persistent lower-third for the full video
- Optional: echo the cover's sparkle or corner-bracket mark near the card as a small brand touch, since that's your established motif

## Outro
- Cut to the Section 1 cover image itself as the closing card — it's already built, already on-brand, zero extra design work
