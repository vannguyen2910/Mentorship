Programme overview

We'll meet 12 times over about three months. You'll bring your conversion-rate redesign from work to our first session, and we'll improve it together week by week. The sessions follow the double diamond in order, so your project goes through the same process you're learning. Developer handoff isn't part of this programme.

You've seen the double diamond before, but it hasn't become part of how you work yet. Taking one project all the way through it should fix that.

We won't spend long on Discover, because research is already your strength. Most of our time goes into Develop, where you build the design system and two prototypes.

Think of this as step one. Strategy and service design can come later. As you put it: "before I learn to fly, I should learn to walk."

AI is part of the programme from Session 1, as an assistant rather than the driver. In most sessions you do the thinking by hand first, then use an AI tool on the same work and check what it gives you. See "How AI Fits In" below for exactly where.

Ready Discover

Prep checklist

  • Lesson: Design Thinking for UX Designer
  • Slides: Deck
  • Homework: Homework
  • Bring a rough story of how the project has gone so far.

Learning objectives

  • Explain the four phases of the double diamond and what each one is for
  • Map your current project onto the double diamond
  • Recognise which phase a piece of past work belongs to
  • Understand who your stakeholders are and what they expect
  • 🤖 Use an AI tool to draft a process overview for your project, then compare it with your own plan
  • 🤖 Set up a simple AI project folder: a short context file about your project (`product-and-users.md`) and three lines of data rules (what you may paste, what you won't, which tool you use for what). About 10 minutes, and every later AI step uses it

Success check

  • Your project is mapped on the double diamond, with today's position marked
  • You can say which phase comes next and why
  • You can name one thing the AI missed about your project
  • Your AI project folder exists, with a context file and your data rules
