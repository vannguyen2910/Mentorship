Programme goal

From strong solo craft → to research-led, evidence-backed, proactively-shared decision-making.

Programme overview

This coaching program is designed to move Tam from Mid-level to Senior/Lead Product Designer over 3 months, using the ERP system-wide UI/UX rebuild as the primary learning vehicle.

Every session combines theory with direct application to the live project: so Tam isn't practising in a vacuum, but building real design artefacts that contribute to the ERP rebuild while closing her skill and behaviour gaps.

Audit & Desk Research

Completed22 Aug 2026Lesson plan →

We covered how to track and prioritise assumptions, then audited the current experience against usability heuristics with AI support. Findings were grouped into patterns that can be presented to stakeholders. She built a reusable heuristic checklist and a stakeholder-ready audit report, and will extend both to every flow.
