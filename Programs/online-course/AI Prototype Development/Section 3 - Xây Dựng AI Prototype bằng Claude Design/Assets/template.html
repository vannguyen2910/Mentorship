About Warorot Night Market
Warorot Market is where Chiang Mai locals actually shop and eat — not the tourist night bazaar. Best after 6pm when the food stalls light up. Go deeper past the flower vendors for the real finds: sticky rice, sausages, and fruit you won't see elsewhere.
